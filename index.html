Our Heritage

Three Generations of Temple Artistry

Since 1948, Madhavan Jewellers has been a name synonymous with South Indian temple jewellery. Our atelier in Chennai carries forward the legacy of master craftsmen who once adorned deities in temples across Tamil Nadu.

Every piece is hand-crafted using traditional techniques passed down through three generations — blending the sacred artistry of temple sculpture with contemporary luxury.
