The Full Madhavan Experience

From bespoke commissions to lifetime care, every service is delivered with the same devotion to craft.

Bespoke Customization

Collaborate with our master designers to create a one-of-a-kind piece. From temple-inspired concept sketches to final polish, we bring your vision to life with rare gemstones and 22K gold.

Expert Repair & Restoration

Bring new life to treasured heirlooms. Our goldsmiths expertly repair, restore, and re-polish pieces of any age — often making them better than new.

Book a one-on-one session with our gemologists and designers. We guide you through gemstone selection, design, and care — no obligation, only expertise.

Bridal Services

Complete bridal jewellery services — from engagement ring design to full temple jewellery suites. Includes complimentary resizing and lifetime cleaning.

Jewellery Cleaning & Care

Complimentary ultrasonic cleaning and professional polishing for all Madhavan pieces. Keep your treasures as radiant as the day you received them.

Certified Appraisals

Independent, insurance-grade appraisals for your fine jewellery. Detailed documentation for insurance, estate, and resale purposes.
