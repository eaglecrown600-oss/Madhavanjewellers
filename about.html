The Madhavan Story

From Temple Walls to Heirloom Chests

It began in 1948, when master goldsmith K. Madhavan opened a modest workshop in T. Nagar, Chennai. His vision was simple yet profound: to bring the sacred artistry of temple sculpture into jewellery that families could wear and cherish.

Three generations later, that vision endures. Every Madhavan piece is still hand-crafted in our atelier, where artisans trained in traditional Nakkasi and Kundan techniques work alongside contemporary designers.

We use only BIS-hallmarked gold and IGI-certified, conflict-free diamonds — because true luxury honours both tradition and conscience.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To preserve and celebrate the art of South Indian temple jewellery, crafting heirlooms that honour life's most sacred moments with uncompromising artistry.

Our Vision

To be India's most treasured jewellery house — where heritage temple craftsmanship and contemporary design meet in perfect harmony, recognised worldwide.

Through the Years

Our Journey

1948

The Atelier Opens

K. Madhavan founds the workshop in T. Nagar, Chennai, crafting temple jewellery for local families.

1972

Second Generation

His son R. Madhavan takes the helm, introducing the first diamond collection while preserving temple traditions.

1995

Bridal Maison

Madhavan becomes Chennai's premier destination for bespoke South Indian bridal jewellery.

2010

National Recognition

Awarded "Master Heritage Artisan" by the Tamil Nadu Crafts Council.

2023

Three Generations On

The third generation expands the atelier while staying true to its temple-inspired roots.
